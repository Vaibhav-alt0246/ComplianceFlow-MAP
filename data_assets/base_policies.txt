CANARA BANK - INTERNAL IT & SECURITY POLICY BASELINE (v2025.4)

SECTION 1: AUTHENTICATION & ACCESS CONTROL
1.1 Password Requirements: All employee, vendor, and administrative system passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters in length, containing at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
1.2 MFA: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory for all internal database access. SMS-based MFA is prohibited; authenticator apps or hardware tokens must be used.
1.3 Session Timeout: All internal dashboard sessions must automatically terminate after 15 minutes of inactivity.

SECTION 2: DATA ENCRYPTION & PRIVACY
2.1 Data at Rest: All Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards, and financial records, must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
2.2 Data in Transit: All internal and external API communications must be secured using TLS 1.3 or higher.

SECTION 3: INCIDENT RESPONSE & AUDITING
3.1 Logging: All system changes, permission updates, and login attempts must be logged in an immutable append-only ledger.
3.2 Reporting Timeline: Any suspected data breach or compliance violation must be reported to the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and the specific Department Head within 24 hours of detection.
3.3 Ticket Creation: All compliance update tasks must be routed through the central IT Service Desk software before deployment.
